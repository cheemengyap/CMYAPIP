传家三关The Legacy 3 Gates

你走了以后，家人拿得到吗？If you were gone tomorrow, could your family reach what you built?

保障做好了，只完成了一半。另一半，是你的家人怎样真正收到它。我用三道关来检查每个家庭的安排。 Protection is only half of the plan. The other half is how your family actually receives it. I check every family against three gates.

有没有遗嘱A valid Will

没有遗嘱的非穆斯林家庭，遗产按 Distribution Act 1958 的法定比例分，不一定是你的心意。Without a Will, a non-Muslim estate follows the Distribution Act 1958, which may not match your wishes.

钱能不能及时到位Liquidity in time

遗产认证过程常见需要数年。家人这段时间的开销与学费，需要提前安排。Probate commonly takes years. Living costs and school fees in that period need to be arranged ahead.

交给谁、怎么给Who gets it, and how

指定受益人与设立信托不同。信托可以控制孩子几时、怎样领取。Naming a nominee and setting up a trust are different. A trust can control when and how a child receives.
